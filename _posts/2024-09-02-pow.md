## Some Thoughts

The Septuagint timeline doesn't appear to correspond to the Prophecy of Weeks, unless the weeks do not all last the same amount of time. The Masoretic timeline matches the Prophecy of Weeks with 700-year weeks, with the understanding that week 2 is not talking about the Flood, but about the death of Adam and Enoch's translation around the same time.

### Issues with the Septuagint

In performing this research, I noticed further issues with the Septuagint timeline:

1. If Abraham's fathers were having children at ages well past 100 -- Nachor is said to have sired Terach at 179! -- then why was Abraham freaking out so much about not having an heir by 100?
2. In the Septuagint chronology Methuselah actually outlives the flood. For the calculations, as well as an explanation as to why this is a problem (Methuselah's own name contains a Flood prophecy), I recommend watching [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5QwAWOt43ow).

### Updates

**Update 1:** I've found out that there is yet another chronology out there - the Samaritan! I've included it for completeness, but it doesn't seem to fit the PoW.

**Update 2:** I've found yet another chronology - the one in Jubilees! It fits the PoW in some ways, but falls short in others. Interestingly, it looks like this may have been the chronology that Paul followed when claiming there were 430 years from the promise to Abraham to the Exodus (see Note 1 above).
